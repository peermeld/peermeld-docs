# Peermeld Launch

**Launch** at [peermeld.com/launch](https://peermeld.com/launch) is Peermeld's weekly product leaderboard — *"Discover products built by the Peermeld community."* Makers submit their products, the community upvotes, and every week crowns its **#1, TOP 2, TOP 3…**

## How the board works

- **Weekly cycles** — the board is organised by week (*Week 30, Week 31, Week 32…*), so every Monday the race resets. Small teams can win their week.
- **Ranked by upvotes** — community members upvote their favourites; ranking badges (**#1 → TOP n**) update as votes accumulate.
- **Product cards** — each entry shows a logo, name, tagline, the maker's avatar/name and category tags like *Productivity, Web App, Social* or *AI, Open Source, DevTools*.

### Real entries from the live board

- 🥇 **Peermeld** — *"The professional network for developers, designers, and creators"* (Productivity, Web App, Social) — 11 upvotes, TOP 1
- 🥈 **Bullshit Detector** — *"Fact-check any video, article or tweet with your AI agent"* (AI, Open Source, Productivity) — TOP 2
- 🥉 **Sluggit** — *"Clean, predictable slug generation for modern TypeScript apps"* (DevTools, Open Source) — TOP 3

## Why launch on Peermeld

1. **A winnable leaderboard** — weekly resets mean you compete with dozens of products, not Product Hunt's thousands per day.
2. **The right audience** — every voter is a builder: developers, designers, founders. Feedback quality is unusually high.
3. **Permanent artifacts** — your launch points at your [project page](../projects), [portfolio](../profile/portfolio) and profile; the SEO equity stays with *you* forever.
4. **Momentum beyond the board** — top products become reference points in the community (*"as seen on Peermeld Launch"*).

## Submitting your product

<Steps>
<Step>

### Prepare your assets
A square logo, a crisp one-line tagline (what it does + for whom), your category tags, and a working live link. The leaderboard shows all of these at a glance.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter the current week
Submit from the Launch page. Your product appears in the running week immediately with ### ranking position and an **Upvote** button.

</Step>
<Step>

### Announce on the Feed
Post your launch to the [Feed](../feed) with the story behind the product — the board shows upvotes; the Feed explains *why* people should care.

</Step>
<Step>

### Work the week
Reply to every comment, fix reported bugs in public, post a mid-week progress update. See the [Launch Playbook](./launch-playbook) for the full campaign plan.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Launching with no warm-up rarely ranks. Spend 1–2 weeks posting build updates on the Feed first
  so the community recognises your name on launch day — [Feed Strategy](../feed/feed-strategy)
  covers exactly this pipeline.
</Callout>
