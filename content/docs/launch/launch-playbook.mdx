# The Launch Playbook

This is the campaign plan for winning your week on [Launch](https://peermeld.com/launch). It assumes a small product (tool, library, app) and a one-person team — exactly who wins Peermeld boards.

## Phase 1 — Warm-up (2 weeks before)

<Steps>
<Step>

### Build in public
Post 3–5 [Feed](../feed) updates about the problem you're solving. Not "my product is coming" — the *problem*. Peers who feel the pain become day-one upvoters.
</Step>
<Step>

### Set up your project page
Create the [project](../projects) with stack tags, screenshots and at least one [build log](../projects/build-logs). The Launch card should click through to depth, not a landing-page shell.
</Step>
<Step>

### Make 20 real friends
Comment thoughtfully on other builders' posts daily. Launch week is when you cash in accumulated goodwill — and Peermeld shows your supporters' [avatars next to upvotes](../feed/engagement), which snowballs.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Phase 2 — Launch week

| Day        | Action                                                                 |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Monday     | Submit early in the weekly cycle; post the launch story to the Feed    |
| Monday     | Share the launch permalink on X/LinkedIn/Discord — import your audience |
| Tue–Wed    | Reply to every comment; ship a quick fix/polish and post about it      |
| Thursday   | Mid-week update: numbers, learnings, a fresh screenshot                |
| Friday     | "Last push" post — transparent, grateful, never spammy                 |
| Weekend    | Thank every upvoter you can find; follow them back                     |

### Upvote ethics that work

- **Ask individuals, not crowds.** A DM/comment to someone you genuinely helped beats a broadcast "please upvote".
- **Never trade votes.** It is visible, it poisons your reputation, and the community is small enough to remember.
- **Earn the leaders.** Early organic upvotes from actual users attract later upvotes from discovery browsers.

## Phase 3 — Compound (the month after)

1. **Write the retrospective log** — "What ranking #2 on Peermeld taught us" is itself great Feed content.
2. **Convert the badge** — screenshot your **TOP n** rank into your [portfolio](../profile/portfolio) and project page; social proof that never expires.
3. **Feed the follow graph** — everyone who engaged gets a follow; they're your next launch's base.
4. **Ship the feedback** — nothing markets a launch like visibly implementing what voters asked for.

<Callout type="idea" title="The meta-strategy">
  Winners treat Launch as a *milestone in a story told continuously on the Feed*, not a
  one-shot event. The board resets weekly; your reputation does not.
</Callout>
