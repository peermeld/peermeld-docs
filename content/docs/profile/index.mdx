# Your Peermeld Profile

Your profile page (`peermeld.com/username`, also served on your [subdomain](../getting-started/claim-subdomain)) is your public professional identity. This page is the complete reference; [setup](./../getting-started/setup-profile) covers the how-to, and [Profile SEO](./profile-seo) the growth angle.

## The page, top to bottom

1. **Banner** — the wide (optionally animated GIF) header image. Prime real estate: use it to show your product, your craft or your personality.
2. **Avatar & identity row** — photo, display name, **company badge** linking to your [company page](../network/companies), `@username`, headline, personal link, country flag and **Joined** date.
3. **Award mark** — notable profiles carry an award/trophy badge, earned through platform achievements.
4. **Follow button** — the single conversion goal of the whole page.
5. **Content tabs** — **Posts**, **Replies**, **Media**.
6. **Portfolio view** — `?view=portfolio` renders your [portfolio blocks](./portfolio) as a dedicated portfolio page.

## Badges and affiliations

- **Company badge** — when you are linked to a company (e.g. the Peermeld team), its avatar appears beside your name on your profile *and* next to all your posts in the Feed. Hover reveals the company; click opens its page. Set it up in [Companies](../network/companies).
- **Award badge** — signals notable achievement on the platform (contributions, milestones). Earned, not bought.
- **Joined date** — "Joined May 2025" tenure is trust you can't fake; another reason to join early.

## Who can see your profile

Everyone — profile pages are public and search-engine indexed (that is the point). Logged-out visitors can read your posts, portfolio and projects; interaction requires an account. That public-by-default design is exactly what makes [Profile SEO](./profile-seo) possible.

## Profile quality checklist

- [ ] Crisp avatar + a banner that shows your work (GIF welcome)
- [ ] Headline with role + niche + audience
- [ ] Bio mentioning your stack and what you're building now
- [ ] One canonical personal link
- [ ] Company badge connected
- [ ] At least 3 strong posts and 1 media-rich post
- [ ] Portfolio blocks filled in
- [ ] Subdomain claimed and matching your @username
