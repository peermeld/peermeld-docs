# Portfolio Blocks & Themes

Peermeld portfolios turn your profile into a visual showcase of real work — **portfolio blocks** for work samples, code snippets and case studies, rendered as a clean site through your profile's **Portfolio view** (`yourname?view=portfolio`) and your subdomain.

## Why portfolio on Peermeld?

Peermeld's homepage pitch says it directly: *"Submit your portfolio and get discovered by potential employers and collaborators."* The portfolio is integrated with everything else — your posts prove activity, your [projects](../projects) prove depth, and the portfolio ties them into one presentation layer employers can browse without logging in.

## Portfolio blocks

**Portfolio Blocks** are Peermeld's dedicated spaces for different kinds of work evidence:

- **Work samples** — shipped designs, apps, articles
- **Code snips** — the clever function, the gnarly query, the elegant component
- **Visual case studies** — problem → process → result, told with images

<Callout type="idea">
  Treat each block as a *story*: context, your specific contribution, and the outcome. Blocks
  named "E-commerce checkout redesign — conversion +18%" beat blocks named "Project 7".
</Callout>

## Themes

Peermeld is rolling out **portfolio themes** — the August 2026 changelog introduced the *first* new theme with **10+ more planned**. Themes restyle your whole portfolio view, so:

1. Pick a theme that matches your craft (minimal for writers, bold for designers, technical for engineers).
2. Re-check new themes as they ship — early adopters stand out while a theme is still rare.
3. Keep your [banner](../getting-started/setup-profile) and avatar visually consistent with the theme.

## Portfolio view

The portfolio view lives at `peermeld.com/yourname?view=portfolio` and on your subdomain. Share it anywhere a traditional portfolio link would go:

- Job applications and freelancer profiles
- Your GitHub README and LinkedIn "Featured" section
- Your email signature next to `yourname.peermeld.com`

## What makes portfolios convert

<Steps>
<Step>

### Lead with your best three
Put the three strongest blocks first — hiring teams rarely scroll past the fold of a portfolio.

</Step>
<Step>

### Show the stack
Use tech-stack tags (React, Node, PostgreSQL…) — the same tags power discovery in [Projects](../projects) and the [directory](../network/directory).

</Step>
<Step>

### Attach live links
A clickable live URL beats a hundred screenshots. Dead links are worse than no link — test monthly.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep it alive
Add a block every time you finish something. A portfolio with this month's work signals momentum; a year-old one signals the opposite. Your [streak](../rewards/streaks) and portfolio should grow together.

</Step>
</Steps>
