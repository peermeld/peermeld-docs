# Quickstart

This is the fastest path from *never heard of Peermeld* to *live profile + first post on the Feed*.

<Steps>
<Step>

## Sign in with Google

Open [peermeld.com](https://peermeld.com) and click **Claim Profile** (or go straight to [peermeld.com/login](https://peermeld.com/login)). Authentication is one click with your Google account — no passwords to invent. Peermeld only reads basic profile information; details are in the [Google data policy](https://peermeld.com/google-data-policy).

</Step>
<Step>

## Claim your subdomain

Use the **Subdomain Checker** on the homepage. Type a name (at least 4 characters, not only numbers) and check if `yourname.peermeld.com` is free. Sign in and it is attached to your profile automatically. This subdomain is your SEO-ready personal URL — see [Claim your Subdomain](./getting-started/claim-subdomain).

</Step>
<Step>

## Complete your profile

Add a **profile photo**, a **banner** (GIFs work great), a one-line **headline** like "Full-stack developer building in public", a short **bio** and your personal website. A complete profile appears in the [/grow directory](https://peermeld.com/grow) and converts visitors into followers.

</Step>
<Step>

## Publish your first post

Go to the [Feed](https://peermeld.com/feed), click the **"What's happening?"** composer and introduce yourself: who you are, what you are building, what you want feedback on. Hit **Post**. Attach an image or drop a link — Peermeld renders rich previews (including YouTube embeds) automatically. See [Creating Posts](./feed/create-post).

</Step>
<Step>

## Engage for five minutes

Upvote three posts you genuinely like, leave one thoughtful comment, and follow five people plus a couple of **Sources** (e.g. freeCodeCamp, Fireship). This seeds your **Following** tab, starts your **streak** and tells the community you have arrived.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="idea" title="Day-one checklist">
  ✅ Account created · ✅ Subdomain claimed · ✅ Profile complete · ✅ First post live · ✅ 3 upvotes + 1 comment + 5 follows. That combination is exactly what starts building your streak, visibility and [rewards](./rewards) from day one.
</Callout>
