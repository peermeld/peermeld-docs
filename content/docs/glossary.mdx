# Glossary

The Peermeld vocabulary, A to Z.

## A

**Award** — A badge on notable profiles marking platform achievements. Earned, not purchased.

## B

**Banner** — The wide header image (GIFs supported) at the top of a profile. [Set up guide](../getting-started/setup-profile).

**Build log** — A markdown write-up attached to a project covering progress, guides and decisions. [Guide](../projects/build-logs).

## C

**Company page** — An organisation profile (`peermeld.com/company/name`) with About, People and Posts. Its logo badges appear beside linked members' names across the site. [Guide](../network/companies).

**Composer** — The "What's happening?" box at the top of the Feed where posts are written.

## D

**Directory** — The professional discovery page at `/grow`, listing members as rich cards with role, headline and country flag. [Guide](../network/directory).

## F

**Feed** — The heart of Peermeld at [peermeld.com/feed](https://peermeld.com/feed): a stream of posts across NEWEST, TRENDING and FOLLOWING tabs with Filters. [Full guide](../feed).

**Filters** — Controls on the Feed that refine which posts appear in your stream.

**Following tab** — The feed timeline showing only posts from accounts you follow.

## G

**Global Search** — Worldwide talent lookup across Peermeld profiles, roles and tech-stack tags. [Guide](../network/peer-match).

## L

**Launch** — The weekly product leaderboard at `/launch` where community products rank by upvotes (#1, TOP 2, TOP 3…). [Guide](../launch).

**Link card** — The rich preview Peermeld renders when a post contains a URL (Open Graph image + title).

## M

**Media tab** — The profile tab collecting all your posts that contain images or attachments.

**Meld Space** — A niche sub-community/forum for specific topics. [Guide](../network/peer-match).

## N

**NEW badge** — A highlight on freshly-joined members' directory cards, giving newcomers instant visibility.

**Newest tab** — The reverse-chronological feed timeline: every post, newest first.

## P

**Peer Match** — Peermeld's pairing system for cross-disciplinary collaboration. [Guide](../network/peer-match).

**Peers Wallet** — Your on-platform wallet where value from the reward system accumulates. [Guide](../rewards/wallet).

**Permalink** — A post's permanent URL (`/post/<id>`), reached by clicking its timestamp.

**Portfolio block** — A showcase unit on your portfolio: work samples, code snips or case studies. [Guide](../profile/portfolio).

**Portfolio view** — The `?view=portfolio` rendering of a profile: your blocks as a clean portfolio site.

**Project** — A structured page for something you built: logo, tagline, stack tags, screenshots, live link, status and build logs. [Guide](../projects).

**Project Room** — A dedicated area for coordinating team-ups and joint ventures. [Guide](../network/peer-match).

## R

**Replies tab** — The profile tab showing your public discussion history.

**Reward system** — The mechanism converting activity, streaks and achievements into tangible value in your Peers Wallet. [Guide](../rewards/wallet).

## S

**Sera Legacy** — The section preserving the technical foundation from Peermeld's original developer roots.

**Source account** — An automated, badged account piping curated content into the feed (e.g. freeCodeCamp `@freecodecamp`, Fireship, Next.js). [Guide](../feed/sources).

**Streak** — Consecutive days of activity on Peermeld; **Top Streaks This Week** is the weekly leaderboard. [Guide](../rewards/streaks).

**Subdomain** — Your free `yourname.peermeld.com` custom domain serving your profile (4+ characters, not only numbers). [Claim guide](../getting-started/claim-subdomain).

## T

**Tech stack tags** — Technology labels on projects (React, Node, PostgreSQL) powering discovery and Global Search. [Guide](../projects).

**Trending tab** — The feed timeline ranking posts by engagement velocity. [How it works](../feed/tabs-and-filters).

## U

**Upvote** — The core appreciation/ranking action on posts; upvoter avatars are displayed on each post. [Guide](../feed/engagement).
