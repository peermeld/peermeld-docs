## Why order matters

Each step multiplies the next one:

1. **Account** unlocks posting, following, upvoting, streaks and the [Peers Wallet](../rewards/wallet).
2. **Subdomain** gives everything you publish a permanent, Google-friendly home.
3. **Profile** converts the traffic your posts and subdomain earn into followers, collaborators and opportunities.

<Callout type="warn">
  Subdomains are first-come, first-served and require at least 4 characters (not only numbers).
  Claim yours *before* sharing your first viral post — your profile URL is what people will search
  for afterwards.
</Callout>
