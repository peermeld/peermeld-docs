# Create Your Account

Peermeld uses **Sign in with Google** as its single authentication method. There are no passwords, no email confirmation loops and no setup wizards — one click and you are in.

## Sign up in 30 seconds

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the login page
Go to [peermeld.com/login](https://peermeld.com/login) — or click **Sign In** in the header of any page, such as the [Feed](https://peermeld.com/feed).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose "Sign in with Google"
Pick the Google account you want associated with your professional identity. If you checked a subdomain on the homepage first, Peermeld will attach it automatically after sign-in, if it is still free.

</Step>
<Step>

### Land on the Feed
You are dropped straight into the Feed's composer ("What's happening?") — the fastest next step is an intro post.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What Google data does Peermeld use?

Peermeld reads only basic profile information from your Google account (such as your name, email and avatar) to create your profile. The full explanation lives in the official [Google data policy](https://peermeld.com/google-data-policy). Your Peermeld profile begins life with your Google name and photo — you can customise everything afterwards in [profile setup](./setup-profile).

<Callout type="info">
  One Google account = one Peermeld identity. Pick the account you already use for your
  professional life so your name and email stay consistent across the network.
</Callout>

## Choosing your public identity

The name on your Google account becomes your initial display name, and you get an **@username** for your profile URL (`peermeld.com/@you`-style paths like `peermeld.com/nazmul`). You can refine your display name, headline and bio right away — see [Set up your profile](./setup-profile).
