# Set Up Your Profile

Your profile is home base: the page your subdomain serves, the page Feed readers click after a great post, and the page recruiters screenshot into hiring threads. Ten focused minutes here pays back for years. A great example to study is the founder's profile at [peermeld.com/nazmul](https://peermeld.com/nazmul).

## Profile anatomy

| Element           | What it is                                                        | SEO & first-impression tips |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| **Banner**        | Wide header image or GIF at the top                                | Show your product or craft in motion; animated GIFs are supported |
| **Avatar**        | Square profile photo                                               | Use a clear face or logo — it appears next to every post and comment |
| **Display name**  | Your real or brand name                                            | Use the name people Google |
| **@username**     | Your handle and profile URL (`peermeld.com/username`)              | Match your subdomain and other handles |
| **Company badge** | Linked company avatar beside your name (e.g. @peermeld)            | Add your company — it links the company page and signals credibility |
| **Headline / bio**| One-liner + short description, e.g. *"Full Stack Developer and Founder & CEO of Peermeld"* | Front-load your role + niche keywords |
| **Link**          | Your personal site (e.g. olova.dev)                                | One canonical link, always current |
| **Country flag**  | Shown from your profile settings in the directory                  | Signals timezone and market for collaborators |
| **Joined date**   | "Joined May 2025" style tenure badge                               | Tenure is free trust — join early |
| **Award**         | Trophy/award mark on notable profiles                              | Awards are earned via platform achievements |
| **Tabs**          | **Posts · Replies · Media** on every profile                       | Keep all three alive; a dead Media tab wastes visual space |
| **Portfolio view**| `?view=portfolio` — your portfolio blocks rendered as a site       | Share this link in job applications |
| **Follow button** | How visitors subscribe to your posts                               | Every post is a funnel to this button |

## The 10-minute setup

<Steps>
<Step>

### Photo and banner
Upload a crisp avatar and a banner. GIF banners are popular on Peermeld and make profiles memorable — pick one that shows *what you do*.

</Step>
<Step>

### Headline
Write one line: role + what you build + audience. *"Frontend engineer crafting delightful web apps"* beats *"Developer"*. The headline shows in the [/grow directory](https://peermeld.com/grow) cards and in Google snippets.

</Step>
<Step>

### Bio
Two to four sentences: who you are, your stack or craft, what you are building now, and who you want to connect with. Real example from the directory: *"Software Engineer | Microservices & Distributed Systems | Event-Driven Architecture | Kafka & gRPC"* — instantly scannable.

</Step>
<Step>

### Link + company
Add your personal site, then link your [company page](../network/companies) so its badge appears on your profile and in your posts.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sanity check on mobile
Open your profile and your subdomain on your phone. That is how most people will first see you.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The three profile tabs

- **Posts** — everything you publish on the [Feed](../feed). Your best content should live here.
- **Replies** — your conversation history. Thoughtful replies are a reputation engine; they stay public.
- **Media** — posts with images/attachments. Pin-worthy screenshots, demos and diagrams end up here automatically.

<Callout type="idea" title="Signal over noise">
  Because all three tabs are public, quality beats quantity. One excellent build-log post per day
  outperforms ten filler posts — for the algorithm, for followers, and for anyone reading your
  Replies tab.
</Callout>

## Keep it fresh

Profiles surface in the **directory**, on **Feed** posts and in search results. Revisit your headline and banner monthly, keep your "currently building" project up to date in [Projects](../projects), and let your [portfolio](./portfolio) grow block by block. Consistency here is also what powers your [streaks](../rewards/streaks).
