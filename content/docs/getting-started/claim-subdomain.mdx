# Claim Your Subdomain

Every Peermeld member can reserve a **free custom subdomain** — `yourname.peermeld.com` — that points at their profile. It is one of Peermeld's signature features: your profile becomes a real personal website with a URL people can remember and Google can rank.

## How to claim it

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Subdomain Checker
On [peermeld.com](https://peermeld.com), find the **"Check if yourname.peermeld.com is free"** section (you can jump straight to `#claim`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Type your name
Enter the subdomain you want. The checker tells you instantly whether it is free with a *"This subdomain is already taken"* style response when it is not.

</Step>
<Step>

### Hit Claim
Click **Claim subdomain** and sign in with Google. Peermeld attaches the subdomain to your profile automatically *if it is still free at that moment* — popular names move fast.

</Step>
<Step>

### Preview your SEO
The claim flow includes an **SEO Preview** showing exactly how your profile will look on Google and other search engines — your title, URL and meta description — before you share it anywhere.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Naming rules

- **Minimum 4 characters** — `sam.peermeld.com` ✅, `sam` works but `sa1` does not.
- **Not only numbers** — `2026.peermeld.com` ❌.
- Subdomains are **first-come, first-served**.
- Keep it short, pronounceable and identical (or close) to your name/handle elsewhere for a consistent personal brand.

<Callout type="idea" title="Pro move">
  Match your subdomain to your GitHub or X handle where possible (`handle.peermeld.com`,
  `github.com/handle`, `x.com/handle`). Identical handles compound: people only have to remember
  one name, and search engines learn to cluster your identities together.
</Callout>

## Why the subdomain matters for SEO

1. **A dedicated crawlable URL** — `yourname.peermeld.com` is a stable address search engines can index independently of the main feed.
2. **Keyword-rich domain** — your name *is* the URL, so searches for your name find you.
3. **Google-ready metadata** — Peermeld renders your name, headline and bio into the page title and meta description, which is what the SEO Preview shows.
4. **A shareable identity** — link it from your GitHub README, X bio and email signature; every backlink strengthens it.

For the full optimisation playbook, read [Profile SEO](../profile/profile-seo).

## Troubleshooting

- **"Already taken" but you want it:** subdomains are not resold; try a sensible variant — add a middle initial, your craft (`nazmul-dev`), or `the-` prefix.
- **Claimed but not loading yet:** give DNS/provisioning a few minutes to propagate, then hard-refresh.
- **Wrong Google account attached:** sign out and redo the claim with the correct account *before* building links to the subdomain.

<Callout type="warn">
  Treat your subdomain as permanent infrastructure. Changing it later means updating every link
  you have ever shared — claim carefully, then build on it for years.
</Callout>
