# Creating Posts

Posting is the core action on Peermeld. This page covers the composer at [peermeld.com/feed](https://peermeld.com/feed) from first keystroke to published permalink.

## Publish your first post

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the composer
Sign in and go to the Feed. The composer sits at the very top with the placeholder **"What's happening?"**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write your update
Type your text. Write naturally — Peermeld posts are plain, readable text where links become live. Long posts collapse behind **Show more** in the stream, so put your hook in the first two lines.

</Step>
<Step>

### Attach media or a link (optional)
Add an image, or paste a URL: Peermeld automatically generates a rich preview (link cards render the target's Open Graph image; YouTube links embed with a playable thumbnail, as seen on feed posts sharing tutorials).

</Step>
<Step>

### Press Post
Your post appears instantly at the top of **NEWEST** and on your profile's **Posts** tab. The timestamp (`1h`, `Aug 10`) is its permanent permalink: `/post/<id>`.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you can put in a post

| Type             | How it renders                                                  | Best for                              |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Plain text       | Clean paragraphs, emoji ✅                                       | Ideas, opinions, questions            |
| URLs             | Auto-linked + rich preview card                                 | Your blog, demo, repo, video          |
| YouTube URLs     | Embedded player thumbnail (e.g. freeCodeCamp tutorial shares)   | Tutorials, talks, demos               |
| Images           | `Post attachment` images served from Peermeld's CDN             | Screenshots, diagrams, launch art     |
| Long-form text   | Collapsed with **Show more** (e.g. the weekly changelog posts)  | Changelogs, build logs, mini-essays   |

<Callout type="warn">
  The composer currently posts plain markdown-ish text — code fences and formatting do not
  render like they do in [build logs](../projects/build-logs). For long formatted content, write
  a build log on a project and *share the link* to the Feed. Links get rich previews, so this is
  the best of both worlds.
</Callout>

## Writing posts that get engagement

Real patterns from the live Trending tab:

1. **Lead with the story, not the link** — *"Brave was getting a bit slow and laggy for me, so I switched to Helium full-time…"* earned 5 upvotes as a personal take before any link appeared.
2. **Ask real questions** — *"Suggest me some open source coding agent IDE…"* invites answers, and answers are engagement.
3. **Share changelogs** — the Peermeld team's own numbered changelog post (131 upvotes) is the most-loved format on the platform: concrete, scannable, honest.
4. **One clear subject per post** — "Gemini 3.5 Pro delayed… again." is a complete post in two lines plus an image.
5. **Attach something visual** — almost every trending post carries an image or embed. Blank walls of text trend less.

## After you post

- **Your profile gets it instantly** — it appears under your **Posts** tab and in followers' **FOLLOWING** tab.
- **Watch upvoter avatars** — Peermeld shows the avatars of users who upvoted your post; these are warm connections — visit their profiles and engage back.
- **Reply to every comment** — early replies double a post's discussion weight and are the single biggest lever for reaching [Trending](./tabs-and-filters).
- **Share the permalink** — the timestamp links to the standalone `/post/<id>` page you can paste anywhere.

<Callout type="idea" title="Streak bonus">
  Posting counts toward your daily activity [streak](../rewards/streaks). One meaningful post a
  day keeps the streak — and the [Top Streaks](../rewards/streaks) leaderboard — alive.
</Callout>
