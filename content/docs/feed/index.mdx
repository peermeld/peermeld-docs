# The Peermeld Feed

The **Feed** at [peermeld.com/feed](https://peermeld.com/feed) is the heartbeat of Peermeld — a live stream of work, ideas, questions, changelogs and launches from developers, designers, founders and creators. If you understand the Feed, you understand Peermeld.

## Anatomy of the feed page

1. **The composer** — at the top: *"What's happening?"* with a **Post** button. This is where every post begins. See [Creating Posts](./create-post).
2. **The tabs** — **NEWEST · TRENDING · FOLLOWING** switch between three different timelines. See [Tabs & Filters](./tabs-and-filters).
3. **Filters** — the **Filters** control refines what appears in your stream.
4. **The stream** — an infinite list of posts from people, companies and [Sources](./sources).

<Callout type="info">
  You can browse the feed logged out, but posting, upvoting, commenting, following, streaks and
  rewards all require a free account — see [Create your account](../getting-started/create-account).
</Callout>

## Anatomy of a post

| Element               | Description                                                                 |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Author header**     | Avatar, display name, company badge (if any), @handle and relative time     |
| **Follow button**     | Follow the author inline, without leaving the feed                          |
| **Content**           | Text with clickable links; long posts collapse behind **Show more**         |
| **Attachments**       | Images and rich link previews — YouTube URLs embed with thumbnails          |
| **Upvote count**      | Number of upvotes, now with **avatars of users who upvoted**                |
| **Comments**          | Threaded replies with their own **Like** and **Reply** actions              |
| **Permalink**         | Click the timestamp (`1h`, `Aug 10`) to open the post page `/post/<id>`     |

## The three timelines

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="NEWEST — the chronological river">
Every post in reverse time order. This is where you discover brand-new members and fresh launches before anyone else. Early upvotes and comments here punch far above their weight — today's newest post can be tonight's trending one.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="TRENDING — what the community values right now">
Posts ranked by recent engagement — upvotes and discussion velocity rather than raw follower counts. This is the meritocracy tab: a first-day member can trend next to the founder. Its changelog post (131 upvotes) is the canonical example of a trending post.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="FOLLOWING — your curated network">
Only posts from people and Sources you follow. Your follow graph is an investment: follow builders in your niche early and this tab becomes the highest-signal professional feed you own.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## What kind of content lives here

Real posts from the live feed, so you know the terrain:

- **Build-in-public updates** — *"Building AtroUI, react based non typical component library…"* (a member's first post, 7 upvotes, link preview included)
- **Official changelogs** — *"Peermeld — This Week's Changelog: portfolio themes, Feed UI redesign, upvoter avatars, Top Streaks, Peers Wallet, reward system…"*
- **News and commentary** — AI releases, framework debates, tool reviews (*"Brave was getting slow, so I switched to Helium full-time"*)
- **Questions to the community** — *"Suggest me some open source coding agent IDE, where I can run multiple agents at the same time…"*
- **Curated content from Sources** — freeCodeCamp tutorials, Fireship videos and more, via [Source accounts](./sources)

## Why the Feed matters to your growth

- **Visibility compounds** — every post can surface in Newest, then Trending, then into followers' tabs and profile visitors' memories.
- **Reputation is public** — your Posts, Replies and Media tabs on your profile are fed by feed activity.
- **Streaks run on activity** — daily posting/engagement feeds your [streak](../rewards/streaks), which feeds the leaderboard and [rewards](../rewards).
- **Launch momentum starts here** — products that rank on [Launch](../launch) almost always warm up their audience on the Feed first.
