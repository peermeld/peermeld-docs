# Engagement on the Feed

Engagement is the feed's currency. On Peermeld it comes in four forms: **upvotes**, **comments** (with likes and replies), **follows** — and the quiet superpower, **upvoter avatars**.

## Upvotes

Every post shows its upvote count and — since the August 2026 changelog — the **avatars of the users who upvoted**. That small UI detail changes the social physics:

- **Upvoting is visible appreciation.** Your avatar literally sits on the posts you support. Authors notice.
- **Upvotes feed Trending.** They are the primary ranking signal for the [TRENDING](./tabs-and-filters) tab.
- **Upvotes are cheap generosity.** One click costs nothing and buys goodwill with the exact people you want to know you.

## Comments, likes and replies

Comments on Peermeld are real threads: each comment has its own **Like** count and **Reply** action, and long threads collapse behind **"See N more comments"**.

What works:

1. **Add information, not applause.** "Great post!" is noise; "I tried this and the uBlock integration cut my tab memory in half" is signal — and gets liked.
2. **Answer questions completely.** When someone asks *"suggest me an open source coding agent IDE…"*, a thorough answer with links earns likes, replies and profile visits.
3. **Use replies to deepen threads.** Replies notify the commenter and restart conversations — the algorithm counts discussion velocity for [Trending](./tabs-and-filters).
4. **Report bugs gracefully in comments.** Members literally post feedback under the founder's posts (*"unable to follow anyone…"*) — the team answers there. It is the fastest support channel that exists.

## Follows

The **Follow** button appears inline on every post card and on every profile. Following someone:

- moves their posts into your [FOLLOWING](./tabs-and-filters) tab,
- signals interest that often converts to a follow-back,
- builds the graph that later powers [Peer Match](../network/peer-match) suggestions for you.

<Callout type="idea" title="The 3-3-3 habit">
  Every day: upvote **3** posts you genuinely liked, write **3** substantive comments, follow **3**
  people whose work you want more of. A month of this transforms your feed, your follower count and
  your streak — without a single piece of self-promotion.
</Callout>

## Sharing posts

Every post has a permalink (click its timestamp) that you can share anywhere — X, LinkedIn, Discord, email. Bring your outside audience *to* your Peermeld posts: their engagement compounds on the platform where your portfolio, projects and streaks live.

## Etiquette that builds reputation

| Do                                                  | Don't                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Upvote generously and visibly                       | Ask for upvote trades                     |
| Credit people when sharing their work               | Paste walls of self-promo links           |
| Reply to early commenters on your posts             | Post-and-ghost                            |
| Keep disagreements about ideas, not people          | Turn threads into flame wars              |
| Report bugs/help requests politely                  | Spam the founder's mentions               |
