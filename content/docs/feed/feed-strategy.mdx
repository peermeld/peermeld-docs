# Feed Strategy

The mechanics are simple (see [Creating Posts](./create-post)). *Strategy* is what separates accounts that grow from accounts that shout into the void. This is the playbook.

## The content mix that works

Aim for a rough weekly mix across four proven post types:

| Type              | Share of your posts | Example from the live feed                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Build updates** | ~40%                | "Building AtroUI… love to share all the progress"           |
| **Takes & news**  | ~25%                | "Turborepo alternatives are getting interesting. Nx, moon…" |
| **Questions**     | ~15%                | "Suggest me some open source coding agent IDE…"             |
| **Changelogs**    | ~20%                | "This Week's Changelog: portfolio themes, wallet, rewards…" |

<Callout type="idea">
  This mix = give, give, give, ask. Builds and changelogs establish credibility; takes spark
  discussion; questions invite the community in. Pure self-promotion or pure questions both stall.
</Callout>

## Cadence & the streak flywheel

- **One meaningful post per day** beats seven posts on Sunday — because daily activity powers your [streak](../rewards/streaks), and streaks put you on **Top Streaks This Week**.
- Consistency also fills your profile's **Posts** and **Media** tabs, making [profile SEO](../profile/profile-seo) work while you sleep.
- Minimum viable routine: **1 post + 3 upvotes + 3 comments + 3 follows** daily (the [3-3-3 habit](./engagement)).

## The 60-minute rule

Trending is velocity. After you post:

1. Stay online for an hour and **reply to every early commenter**.
2. **Upvote** the comments — likes on comments count as visible appreciation.
3. If the post links to your work, answer support-ish questions fast (the founder answers bug reports in comments — take the hint).
4. Share the **permalink** to one outside channel (X, Discord) while it is fresh to import early engagement.

## What kills reach

- **Link-only posts with no take** — cards unfurl, but nobody upvotes a bare URL.
- **Walls of text with no visual** — attach a screenshot or embed; the live Trending tab proves visuals win.
- **Posting and vanishing** — zero replies to your own thread tells the algorithm the conversation is dead.
- **Copy-paste crossposting** — write natively for Peermeld's builder audience; they can smell recycled LinkedIn content.

## Measure by observation

Until analytics dashboards arrive, instrument yourself:

- **Track upvotes and upvoter avatars** per post — who upvotes tells you *which audience* you are reaching.
- **Watch which of your posts get comments vs. upvotes** — comments indicate discussion-worthy topics; double down there.
- **Note follower count weekly** — correlate with cadence changes.
- **Screenshot your Trending appearances** — they are portfolio evidence, literally.

## Compounding plays

Once the basics are weekly habits, layer these:

1. **Feed → Launch pipeline:** tease the product for 2–3 weeks on the Feed, then enter [Launch](../launch) with a warm audience.
2. **Feed → Portfolio pipeline:** every good post screenshot becomes a portfolio block case study.
3. **Feed → Company page:** repost your best updates via your [company page](../network/companies) for double surface area.
4. **Feed → Subdomain SEO:** popular posts strengthen your profile, which is the page `yourname.peermeld.com` serves — see [Profile SEO](../profile/profile-seo).
