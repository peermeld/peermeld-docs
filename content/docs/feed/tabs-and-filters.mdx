# Feed Tabs & Filters

The Feed offers three timelines — **NEWEST**, **TRENDING**, **FOLLOWING** — plus **Filters**. Each answers a different question, and power users switch between them deliberately.

## The three tabs

<Tabs items={["NEWEST", "TRENDING", "FOLLOWING"]}>
<Tab value="NEWEST">

**The firehose, in reverse chronological order.**

- Every public post, newest first — from fresh faces to official changelog updates.
- *Use it for:* discovery. Finding new builders, brand-new launches and unanswered questions before the crowd.
- *Strategic value:* being the first thoughtful commenter on tomorrow's trending post.

</Tab>
<Tab value="TRENDING">

**What the community is rewarding right now.**

- Posts surface based on recent engagement — upvotes, and the velocity and quality of discussion.
- Real examples: the weekly Peermeld changelog hit the top with 131 upvotes; build-in-public intros and hot tech news trend daily.
- *Use it for:* understanding what the community values, and getting your best work maximum reach.
- *Strategic value:* a trending post pulls profile visits, follows and subdomain clicks for days.

</Tab>
<Tab value="FOLLOWING">

**Just the people and Sources you chose.**

- A clean, high-signal stream of everyone you follow — colleagues, role models and content [Sources](./sources) like freeCodeCamp or Fireship.
- *Use it for:* daily reading without noise, and nurturing the relationships that lead to collaborations.
- *Strategic value:* every follow you give shapes this tab; every follow you *earn* places you in someone else's.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## What makes a post trend

Peermeld does not publish its exact ranking formula, but observable behavior and official changelog notes tell us the core inputs:

1. **Upvotes** — the headline engagement number on every post card, with avatars of upvoters now displayed.
2. **Discussion** — comments with likes and replies signal a conversation worth surfacing.
3. **Recency** — trending is a *velocity* game: fast early engagement beats slow accumulation.
4. **Community identity, not size** — first-week members trend next to the founder when their content resonates.

<Callout type="info" title="Practical consequence">
  The first 60 minutes of a post decide its trending fate. Post when you can stick around to
  reply, and give your early commenters great answers — back-and-forth discussion is exactly
  what the algorithm is looking for.
</Callout>

## Filters

The **Filters** control on the Feed refines the stream beyond the three tabs. Use it when you are hunting for a specific kind of content — e.g. media-rich posts (screenshots, demos) or specific topics — instead of scrolling the raw river.

## A daily browsing ritual

<Steps>
<Step>

**FOLLOWING first (5 min)** — engage with your circle: upvote, reply, encourage. This is relationship maintenance that pays back on your own posts.

</Step>
<Step>

**NEWEST second (5 min)** — welcome new members and catch fresh launches early. Early upvoters get remembered (their avatar sits on the post).

</Step>
<Step>

**TRENDING last (5 min)** — learn from what worked today and join the biggest conversations while they are hot.

</Step>
</Steps>

Fifteen minutes through this loop maintains your [streak](../rewards/streaks), grows your network and keeps your finger on the community's pulse — all before your coffee cools.
