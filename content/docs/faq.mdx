# Frequently Asked Questions

Fast answers to everything people ask about Peermeld. Can't find yours? Post it on the [Feed](https://peermeld.com/feed) — the founder literally answers product questions in comments.

## About Peermeld

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="What is Peermeld?">
Peermeld is a professional network for developers, designers, founders and creators to showcase real work, find collaborators and launch ideas. It combines a social Feed, SEO-ready profile subdomains, portfolios, project showcases, a weekly product Launch leaderboard, a talent directory, and a reputation economy of streaks, a Peers Wallet and rewards.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is Peermeld free?">
Yes — signing up, posting, claiming a yourname.peermeld.com subdomain, building a portfolio and projects, and launching products are all free.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Who can join Peermeld?">
Anyone whose work helps great work happen. The community skews developer-heavy, but designers, founders, writers, students and other professionals are explicitly welcome.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How is Peermeld different from LinkedIn?">
Peermeld is work-first rather than title-first: your feed posts, projects, build logs, launches and streaks are the profile. Plus you get a free personal subdomain, a build-in-public culture and a young leaderboard you can actually top. See the blog category Comparisons & Alternatives for deep dives.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Accounts & Subdomains

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="How do I sign up?">
Go to peermeld.com/login and use Sign in with Google — one click, no passwords. See Create Your Account.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What Google data does Peermeld use?">
Basic profile info (name, email, avatar) to create your account. Full details: peermeld.com/google-data-policy.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I claim myname.peermeld.com?">
Use the Subdomain Checker on the homepage, enter a name (4+ characters, not only numbers), click Claim, and sign in — it attaches to your profile automatically if still free. Full guide: Claim your Subdomain.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I change my subdomain later?">
Treat it as permanent — subdomains are first-come first-served and links accumulate. Choose carefully and build on it long-term.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## The Feed

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="What are the three feed tabs?">
NEWEST shows every post in reverse-chronological order; TRENDING surfaces posts with hot engagement velocity; FOLLOWING shows only people and Sources you follow. Details: Tabs & Filters.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I post images or videos?">
Attach an image via the composer, or paste a link — URLs unfurl into rich preview cards and YouTube links embed playable thumbnails. See Creating Posts.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What are Source accounts?">
Automated, badged accounts that pipe quality content into the feed — freeCodeCamp, Fireship, Chrome Developer, Astro, Next.js, Node.js and the Peermeld system account. They're labelled sources and are followable like any profile.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How does Trending work?">
By engagement velocity: upvotes and active discussion shortly after posting. Exact weights aren't public, but early upvotes + real comment threads are the proven inputs.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why do I see avatars next to upvote counts?">
A recent update shows the avatars of users who upvoted a post — appreciation is now visible, which makes upvoting a small act of relationship-building.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Profiles, Portfolios & Projects

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="What are the Posts / Replies / Media tabs?">
Every profile has three public tabs: your posts, your reply history, and all your posts with images. All three shape your reputation — see Your Profile.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What is the ?view=portfolio link?">
It renders your portfolio blocks as a dedicated portfolio page — share it in job applications. Themes restyle it, with 10+ themes planned. See Portfolio Blocks & Themes.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What are build logs?">
Markdown write-ups attached to projects — progress, guides, decisions — written in a rich editor with instant preview. See Build Logs.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do tech stack tags help me?">
Tags (React, Node, PostgreSQL…) on projects make you findable in Global Search by recruiters and collaborators filtering for specific stacks.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Launch

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="How does the weekly Launch leaderboard work?">
Submit your product during a week, the community upvotes, and products rank #1, TOP 2, TOP 3… The board resets weekly, so small teams can win. See Peermeld Launch.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="When should I launch?">
After 1–2 weeks of build-in-public posts so the community recognises you. The Launch Playbook has the full campaign plan.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Streaks, Wallet & Rewards

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="What is a streak on Peermeld?">
Consecutive days of activity (posting, commenting, upvoting, shipping). Top Streaks This Week showcases the most consistent members weekly. See Streaks.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What is the Peers Wallet?">
Your on-platform wallet where value earned through the reward system accumulates. It's new and evolving — watch the changelog for expansions.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do rewards work?">
Daily activity, streaks, launches and achievements flow into the reward system, which credits your Peers Wallet. The design rewards genuine contribution, not volume farming.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Growing on Peermeld

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="How do I get my first 100 followers?">
Complete profile + daily posts + the 3-3-3 habit (3 upvotes, 3 comments, 3 follows daily) + one launched product. Details: Feed Strategy.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I find collaborators?">
Directory (/grow), Peer Match, Meld Spaces and Project Rooms — covered in Network & Discovery.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do companies use Peermeld?">
Create a company page, link team members (your logo appears beside all their posts), publish changelog posts and back employee launches. See Company Pages.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where do I report bugs or request features?">
Comment on the official Peermeld posts in the Feed — the team answers there, publicly and fast.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
