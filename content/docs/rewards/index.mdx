# Streaks, Wallet & Rewards

Peermeld doesn't just host your work — it **pays attention to your consistency**. Three connected systems, all introduced or improved in the August 2026 changelog, form the platform's reputation economy: **Streaks**, the **Peers Wallet**, and the **Reward System**.

## Why Peermeld built an economy

Professional networks have a cold truth: visibility goes to those who already have it. Peermeld's systems invert that — they reward **behaviour any member can choose daily** (posting, engaging, shipping) rather than pre-existing fame:

- **Show up daily** → streak grows → you appear on Top Streaks This Week
- **Create and engage** → rewards accrue → your wallet reflects real contribution
- **Ship and launch** → ranks and awards → your profile gains permanent credibility

## The golden rules

1. **Consistency beats intensity** — 15 minutes daily outperforms 5 hours on Sunday (the streak logic literally encodes this).
2. **Genuine activity only** — the systems measure real posting, engagement and shipping; farming low-quality posts backfires socially and algorithmically.
3. **Everything compounds** — streaks feed visibility, visibility feeds follows, follows feed launch upvotes, launches feed reputation. There is no single lever; there is a flywheel.
