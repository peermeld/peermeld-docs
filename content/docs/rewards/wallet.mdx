# Peers Wallet & Rewards

The August 2026 changelog introduced two connected features: the **Peers Wallet** and the **reward system**. Together they are Peermeld's way of turning *reputation into something tangible* — your consistent building and engagement accrue value you can actually hold.

## The Peers Wallet

The **Peers Wallet** is your on-platform wallet — a single place that shows what you've earned through the reward system. Think of it as the ledger of your reputation economy:

- **Earned, not bought** — wallet value comes from activity and achievement, reflecting real contribution
- **Visible and cumulative** — a public-adjacent record that grows with your consistency
- **Foundation for more** — wallets exist to eventually *do* things; Peermeld is building the spending side over time

<Callout type="info">
  The wallet and reward mechanics are among Peermeld's newest features and are actively
  evolving. This page tracks the official [changelog](../changelog) — check it after big
  changelog posts for updates.
</Callout>

## How rewards flow

```text
Daily activity (posts, comments, upvotes)
        +  Consistency (streaks)
        +  Achievements (launches, trending posts, awards)
        →  Reward system
        →  Peers Wallet balance grows
```

### What to focus on

| Behaviour                     | Why it pays                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Daily posting & engagement    | Core input to streaks and rewards              |
| Hitting [Top Streaks](./streaks) | Weekly visibility + milestone recognition   |
| Ranking on [Launch](../launch) | High-value achievements, community-wide proof |
| Helpful comments & answers    | Social capital the system is designed to notice |
| Keeping content quality high  | Rewards follow *valued* activity, not volume   |

## Position yourself early

Platform economies reward their earliest steady participants most — the same way early GitHub contributors or early Stack Overflow answerers still enjoy outsized reputations today. The playbook:

1. **Start your streak now** — history can't be backdated.
2. **Pile up achievements** — launches, trending posts, awards are the supply shocks of the economy.
3. **Watch the changelog** — reward mechanics will expand; early adopters of new reward categories benefit first.
4. **Stay genuine** — economies die from farming; Peermeld's small-community stage makes authenticity visible and valuable.
