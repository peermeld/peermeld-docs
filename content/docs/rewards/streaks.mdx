# Streaks & Top Streaks

A **streak** counts consecutive days of genuine activity on Peermeld. **Top Streaks This Week** — added in the August 2026 changelog, *"still under development"* and being improved — is the weekly showcase of the platform's most consistent members.

## What counts toward a streak

Activity means *participating*, not lurking:

- **Posting** on the [Feed](../feed) — the clearest streak action
- **Commenting and replying** in discussions
- **Upvoting** and engaging with others' work
- **Shipping** — project updates, [build logs](../projects/build-logs), portfolio additions

<Callout type="info">
  Exact streak rules are evolving (the team says improvement work is ongoing). The safe strategy
  that always counts: **publish or meaningfully engage at least once every day**.
</Callout>

## Why streaks matter more than you think

1. **The weekly leaderboard** — Top Streaks This Week puts consistent members in front of the whole community, no followers required.
2. **Trust on sight** — a visible streak signals reliability; collaborators and employers read it as "this person finishes things".
3. **The forcing function** — streaks make you post daily, and daily posting is what fills your [profile tabs](../profile), feeds the [algorithm](../feed/tabs-and-filters) and grows your [profile SEO](../profile/profile-seo). The streak is the wrapper around the real growth.
4. **Wallet synergy** — consistent activity is the input to the [reward system](./wallet).

## Building (and protecting) a streak

<Steps>
<Step>

### Anchor it to a habit
Pair Peermeld with morning coffee or your end-of-day shutdown ritual. Streaks die from forgetting, not from laziness.
</Step>
<Step>

### Keep a ideas backlog
Draft post ideas in a note (bugs hit, things learned, hot takes). On empty-brain days, publish from the backlog.
</Step>
<Step>

### Minimum-viable day plan
Busy day? One thoughtful comment + three upvotes takes 4 minutes. Keep the flame alive; write the essay tomorrow.
</Step>
<Step>

### Review weekly
Check Top Streaks This Week every week. Study who is on it — consistent members are the best people to follow and learn from.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Streak psychology that works

- **Never miss twice** — one lost day is an accident; two is a new (bad) habit.
- **Don't worship the number** — a 60-day streak of filler helps no one; a 20-day streak of real posts changes careers.
- **Publicly commit** — post "day 30 of shipping daily" updates; the community loves streak milestones and upvotes them generously.
