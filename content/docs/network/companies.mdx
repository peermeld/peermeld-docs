# Company Pages

Organisations live on Peermeld too. A **company page** (`peermeld.com/company/yourname`) gives your team a public home that links to every member who works there — and puts your logo beside their every post.

## Anatomy of a company page

Using the official example, [peermeld.com/company/peermeld](https://peermeld.com/company/peermeld):

| Element        | Example                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Logo**       | The Peermeld mark                                             |
| **Name & handle** | *Peermeld — @peermeld · organization*                      |
| **Tagline**    | *"The professional network for developers, designers, founders and creators…"* |
| **Facts row**  | *2–10 people · 0 on Peermeld · remote · Website*              |
| **About tab**  | *"Who we are"* narrative section                              |
| **People**     | Team grid: *Nazmul Hossain — Admin — Software Engineer @ Peermeld* |
| **Posts tab**  | The company's feed posts                                      |

## The badge effect

Once a member is linked to your company:

- Your **logo appears beside their name** on their profile and **next to every post they publish** in the Feed — every team member becomes a distribution channel.
- Hovering the badge shows the company name; clicking opens your page.
- Your *People* section grows into a browsable, verifiable team roster — quiet, powerful social proof for recruiting.

## Setting up your company

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the page
Add your organisation with its name, @handle, logo and tagline. Mark it as an **organization** profile.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill the facts
Size (e.g. *2–10 people*), work model (*remote* / hybrid / on-site), and website. These are filterable facts job-hunters scan first.

</Step>
<Step>

### Write "Who we are"
Two short paragraphs: what you build, and who thrives there. Skimmable, honest, no buzzword salad.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add your people
Link team members with roles (**Admin**, Software Engineer…). They get the badge; you get the distribution.

</Step>
<Step>

### Post as the company
Ship changelogs and announcements from the company account — the Peermeld team's own weekly changelog (top of Trending with 131 upvotes) shows exactly how effective company posting is.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Company page strategy

- **Weekly changelog ritual** — it worked for Peermeld itself; numbered, concrete updates build trust and follows.
- **Amplify employee launches** — when a team member enters [Launch](../launch), back them from the company page.
- **Recruit through work** — companies whose people visibly ship attract inbound applications without job boards.
