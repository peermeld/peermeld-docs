# The Directory (/grow)

[peermeld.com/grow](https://peermeld.com/grow) — titled *"Discover Professionals and Grow Your Network"* — is Peermeld's browsable member directory. It exists for one purpose: helping people **discover professionals with complementary interests and skills** to collaborate, exchange knowledge and grow together.

## How directory cards work

Each member appears as a rich card:

- **Banner + avatar** — your visual identity at card size
- **Name, role and country flag** — *Software Engineer · 🇧🇩*
- **Headline bio** — the one-liner that decides whether someone clicks
- **NEW badge** — freshly joined members are highlighted, so new users get instant visibility
- **View Profile button** — the click-through to your full page
- **Source/system labels** — [Source accounts](../feed/sources) like freeCodeCamp, Fireship, Chrome Developer, Astro, Next.js and Node.js are listed too, clearly labelled

Real cards from the live directory: full-stack founders, microservices engineers (*"Kafka & gRPC"*), frontend devs dedicated to amazing UX, Gen AI engineers — all discoverable by headline keywords in seconds.

## Finding the right people

<Steps>
<Step>

### Scan by role first
Roles are displayed prominently (*Full-Stack Developer*, *Software Engineer*). Skim for the discipline you need.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read headlines, not just titles
*"Backend-heavy but happy across the stack"* vs *"React specialist"* — the headline tells you fit before you ever click.

</Step>
<Step>

### Use flags for timezone-fit
The country flag is a quiet superpower for finding collaborators in compatible timezones.

</Step>
<Step>

### Click through and read the Media tab
A picture of shipped work is worth ten headlines — [profile tabs](../profile) tell you who actually builds.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Making your card worth clicking

The directory is a marketplace of first-glance judgments. Optimise the four things every card shows:

1. **Banner** — show your product, not a default gradient.
2. **Role field** — use the standard term people search (*Frontend Developer*, not *Code Ninja*).
3. **Headline** — role + specialty + proof, e.g. *"Full-stack dev building SaaS for educators; 2 launches on Peermeld"*.
4. **Freshness** — active members with visible [streaks](../rewards/streaks) and recent posts convert dramatically better than dormant profiles.

<Callout type="warn">
  An incomplete profile in the directory is worse than an absent one — it is your one-shot first
  impression with exactly the people looking for someone like you. Finish
  [profile setup](../getting-started/setup-profile) before you network hard.
</Callout>
