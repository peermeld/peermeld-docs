## How discovery actually happens on Peermeld

Four converging streams bring people to you:

1. **The Feed** — great posts pull profile visits; every post carries your Follow button and company badge.
2. **The Directory** — [peermeld.com/grow](https://peermeld.com/grow) lists members as rich cards: banner, avatar, name, role, headline, flag. Completeness wins clicks.
3. **Search & stack tags** — Global Search plus [project tech tags](../projects) surface you for "React + PostgreSQL" style queries.
4. **Reputation systems** — [Top Streaks](../rewards/streaks), [Launch](../launch) ranks and awards put you in front of thousands weekly.

<Callout type="idea">
  Network effects on Peermeld are *earned through work*, not claimed through titles. A member
  with three shipped projects and a 30-day streak outranks a VP title every time.
</Callout>

## Networking etiquette

- **Give before you ask.** Upvote and comment on someone's work before DM-ing a request.
- **Be specific.** "I saw your Kafka post — we're hitting the same consumer-lag issue" opens doors; "let's connect" opens nothing.
- **Use the public channel first.** Feed comments build shared context that private messages can't.
- **Collaborate visibly.** Project Rooms and shipped collaborations are the strongest possible follow magnets.
