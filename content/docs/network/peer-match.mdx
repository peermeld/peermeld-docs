# Peer Match, Meld Spaces & Project Rooms

Beyond the public directory, Peermeld ships dedicated collaboration features — together they form the platform's "find your people" engine.

## Peer Match

**Peer Match** pairs professionals for cross-disciplinary collaboration. Where a directory makes *you* search, Peer Match brings complementary people to you — the backend engineer for your frontend, the designer for your developer-tool launch.

<Callout type="idea">
  Match quality tracks profile quality: complete your [stack tags](../projects),
  [headline and bio](../getting-started/setup-profile), and keep your posts honest about what
  you're looking for. The algorithm can only match what it can see.
</Callout>

## Meld Spaces

**Meld Spaces** are niche sub-communities — forums tailored to specific topics (a framework, an industry, a craft). Think deeper, smaller rooms instead of the whole-house Feed:

- Find Spaces adjacent to your stack and introduce yourself with a specific question or resource.
- Regular, valuable contributions in one Space build dense reputation fast — the same people see you repeatedly.
- Cross-post your best Space discussion back to the main Feed for wider discovery.

## Project Rooms

**Project Rooms** coordinate team-ups and joint ventures — the dedicated area for turning "we should build that" into assigned, shipped work:

1. Post the idea with a concrete scope (2-week micro-tools beat vague platforms).
2. Recruit from your [feed network](../feed/feed-strategy) and the [directory](./directory).
3. Use the room for coordination; use [build logs](../projects/build-logs) for public progress.
4. Ship it to [Launch](../launch) — Rooms-to-Launch is Peermeld's most complete collaboration arc.

## Global Search

**Global Search** lets clients, partners and recruiters look up talent worldwide instantly. It leans on everything in these docs: names, roles, headlines, [tech-stack tags](../projects), [portfolio blocks](../profile/portfolio) and activity. There is no search console to trick — the only strategy is complete, keyword-true, active profiles.

## Sera Legacy

Peermeld's tech roots run through **Sera Legacy** — the section carrying over the technical foundation from the platform's original developer roots. It is a nod to where the network came from: built by developers, for people who build.

## Pulling it together

```text
Directory (browse)  +  Peer Match (be found)  +  Meld Spaces (belong)
        +  Project Rooms (build together)  +  Global Search (be hired)
        =  Peermeld's full collaboration engine
```

Feed your work into it daily — [streaks](../rewards/streaks) make sure the engine never forgets you exist.
